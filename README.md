# Data Availability

Due to copyright, privacy, and portrait-right considerations, only a subset of the dataset is publicly available at this stage.

In the `example` folder, we provide representative examples for **8 of the 10 poses** included in our dataset.

In the `partial data` folder, we provide a subset of the **acoustic imaging results for one representative pose**, allowing users to inspect the format and characteristics of the processed data.

The remaining data are currently withheld to protect participant privacy and associated rights. The full dataset is intended to be released upon acceptance, subject to obtaining the necessary participant consent and applicable usage permissions.
